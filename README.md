# Speaker Sync

This repository hosts Speaker Sync release downloads. The source code is not public.

Download Speaker Sync from the [Speaker Sync website](https://store.standfaststudios.com/speakersync/download/) or from the [latest release](https://github.com/StandfastStudios/SpeakerSync/releases/latest).

Speaker Sync plays your Mac's audio in sync on AirPlay, Google Cast, Fire TV and Roku speakers and TVs around your home. It needs macOS 14.4 or later.

The first time you open it, macOS says it can't check the app for malicious software. Click Done, then open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway** next to Speaker Sync. You only need to do this once.

Speaker Sync is commercial software. It's free to try for 10 days, with every feature and no account or card needed. Then it's $5.49 USD once (plus any sales tax or VAT), a lifetime license for Speaker Sync 1 and every 1.x update on up to two Macs.
